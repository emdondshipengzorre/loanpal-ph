## Full Description (4000 chars max)

LoanPal PH is your personal Filipino finance companion — track every loan, bill, and payment in one place. Whether you're managing lending app loans from Tala, HomeCredit, GCash Credit, or BillEase, or keeping up with Meralco, PLDT, and other monthly bills, LoanPal PH keeps you organized and on top of your finances.

**LOAN TRACKING**
- Track loans from 70+ popular Philippine lending apps
- Monitor remaining balances, monthly payments, and due dates
- Visual progress bars show how close you are to paying off each loan
- Record payments with one tap and see your balance update instantly

**BILL MANAGEMENT**
- Organize bills by category: Utilities, Telecom, Housing, Insurance, Subscriptions
- Track popular providers: Meralco, PLDT, Globe, Manila Water, and more
- Mark bills as paid each period — never miss a payment

**SMART ALERTS & REMINDERS**
- Get notified before due dates so you never pay late
- Color-coded urgency system: overdue (red), due today (orange), due soon (yellow)
- Quick-pay cards on the home screen for items due within 3 days

**FINANCIAL HEALTH SCORE**
- Proprietary health score from 0-100 based on your payment habits
- Visual breakdown showing what's helping and hurting your score
- Track your progress over time as you improve your financial health

**DEBT PAYOFF STRATEGIES**
- Snowball method: pay off smallest balances first for quick motivation wins
- Avalanche method: pay off highest-cost loans first to save the most money
- See projected payoff timelines and total amounts paid
- Add extra budget to see how much faster you can be debt-free

**PAY-DAY PLANNER**
- Organize payments by payday period (1st-15th and 16th-31st)
- See exactly what you owe each payday cycle
- Plan your budget around your actual pay schedule

**OCR DOCUMENT SCANNING**
- Scan loan statements and receipts with your camera
- On-device text recognition — your images stay private
- Auto-detects amounts, due dates, and lending app names
- Batch import multiple loans from a single scan

**EXPORT & REPORTS**
- Export payment history as a PDF document
- Share proof-of-payment records easily
- Complete loan and bill payment history at your fingertips

**CLOUD SYNC & SECURITY**
- Sync your data across multiple devices
- Biometric lock (fingerprint/Face ID) protects your financial data
- Secure authentication via phone OTP or Google sign-in
- Works offline — changes sync automatically when back online

**QUICK PAYMENT LINKS**
- One-tap shortcuts to GCash and Maya for fast payments
- Native calendar integration for due date reminders

LoanPal PH is 100% free with no ads. Your financial data stays private — we never sell or share it. Built specifically for the Filipino lending landscape.

Download now and take control of your finances!
