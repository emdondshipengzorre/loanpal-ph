Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

LoanPal PH ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal finance management application designed for Filipino users to track loans, bills, and payments. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your phone number or Google account email address for authentication purposes.

Financial Data You Enter: Loan details (lender, amounts, due dates), bill information (provider, amounts, due dates), and payment records. This data is entered manually by you or via our OCR scanning feature.

Device Information: We may collect basic device identifiers for crash reporting and app performance monitoring.

Camera Data: When you use the OCR scan feature, images are processed on-device using ML Kit. Images are not uploaded to our servers or any third party.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's core functionality (loan/bill tracking, payment recording, health score calculation)
  • To sync your data across devices via our cloud service (Supabase)
  • To send push notifications for payment reminders (with your permission)
  • To generate payment history exports when you request them

3. Data Storage and Security

Your financial data is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage, and optionally synced to our cloud database (hosted on Supabase with row-level security). Authentication tokens are stored in your device's secure keystore (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android).

We use industry-standard security practices including HTTPS encryption for all data transfers and row-level security policies ensuring you can only access your own data.

4. OCR Processing

Our document scanning feature uses Google ML Kit for on-device text recognition. All image processing happens locally on your device. We do not upload, store, or transmit your scanned images to any server.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Cloud database and authentication (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Google ML Kit: On-device OCR text recognition (ML Kit Terms)
  • Expo/EAS: App build and update infrastructure (expo.dev/privacy)

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal or financial data with third parties. Your data is only accessible to you through your authenticated account.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data within the app at any time.
  • Export: You can export your payment history as a PDF document.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data from the Settings screen. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.
  • Correction: You can edit or update any loan or bill information at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

LoanPal PH is not intended for use by children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
